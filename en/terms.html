What this website keeps

This website uses no cookies, no advertising and no third-party analytics, and it has no forms. The only thing it keeps is a technical log of each visit, as any web server does, with this data.

  • The date and time.
  • The page opened, which of its sections are seen, which links or buttons are pressed (for example, the CV download) and how long it stays open.
  • The IP address.
  • The approximate country and city or region, worked out from the IP.
  • The browser, the type of device and the language, and the technical connection data the browser sends.
  • The website you came from, if your browser says so.

What for

To know how many people visit the website, how they get here and which pages interest them, and to detect abuse and attacks. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or handed to anyone.

The legal basis is the legitimate interest in keeping the website secure and knowing how it is used (Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation).

Where it is kept and for how long

The log lives on my own server inside the European Union, in a folder only I can access. It is kept for 24 months at most.

The website reaches your browser through Cloudflare, which acts as a technical intermediary to protect it and serve it faster, and handles the connection under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask me to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict its use or to object to it by writing to fedestalgat@gmail.com. To find your visits in the log I will need the approximate date and your IP address.

If you think I have not handled your request properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

If I change anything in these terms, you will see it here with its new date.

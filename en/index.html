I'm Federico Lora, a Higher Technician in Network Systems Administration (ASIR), and I work as a systems technician at CleverByte Consulting in Córdoba, on a permanent morning contract. In the afternoons I'm finishing my Higher Degree in Multiplatform Application Development (DAM).

I do two things that fit together: cross-platform app development — mostly with Flutter, for Android, web and Windows from a single codebase — and setting up the servers they run on: Linux, Docker, PostgreSQL and Cloudflare, with no open ports and with backups.

Since February 2026 I've taken my own and client projects to production: the Guadalupe app for the 2026 pilgrimage; the app of the World Organisation of Cursillos in Christianity; the website of the Génesis cultural association; and Faiten, my biggest personal project.

I come from systems — servers, networks, hardware and cybersecurity at Hospital Cruz Roja, Magtel and an Erasmus+ placement in Lithuania — and it shows in how I work: documented, tested and secure from day one.

The phrase that defines me best: “Excellence is not an act, but a habit”.

Frequently asked questions

What is a cross-platform app?

An app that is written once and runs on Android, on the web and on Windows. I build them with Flutter, Google's framework: one codebase, lower cost and the same features on every device.

Can you make an app that works on iPhone?

Yes: as an installable web app (PWA) that opens from Safari and goes on the home screen like any other app. If it also has to be on the App Store, we look at it in the quote.

How much does it cost and how long does it take?

It depends on what you need. I prepare a fixed, written quote with what's included and the deadlines, and the first conversation is free.

Do you only work in Córdoba?

No. In Córdoba I can come in person when needed, and remotely I work for anywhere in Spain and abroad, in Spanish or English.

Do you also take care of the server, the domain and maintenance?

Yes. I set up the server with Docker and Cloudflare, configure the domain and email, put encrypted backups in place and, if you want, I stay on for maintenance.

Got a project in mind?

Tell me about it and I'll prepare a no-obligation quote.
